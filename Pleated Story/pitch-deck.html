05 / 15 · Where the headroom is

Six numbers worth a closer look

You already hit the better number. For ten weeks straight.

From the start of June to the first week of August, Pleated Story converted between 48% and 56% of every checkout session, against a category median of 37.8%. Then a Facebook push brought nearly five times the usual traffic, delivered the best week of the year, and converted at 33%. That is the whole story of this slide: the store sells beautifully to the people who already know it, and it has not yet been built to hold that standard when a colder crowd arrives — or to sell a second piece to the crowd that does buy.

01

The week that cost the most was your biggest week

For ten weeks the checkout ran at 48–56%. In the week of 10 August a Facebook push brought 932 sessions — 4.7× the prior four weeks' average — and produced your best week of the year, 309 orders. It also converted 33.2%, because Facebook traffic completes payment at 44.5% where Instagram completes at 55.9%. The spend worked. The store just wasn't set up to catch the colder half of what it bought.

01

Where is the category moving?

Indian contemporary ethnic is moving away from the one-off occasion buy toward separates that recombine — kurtas, co-ords and sets a customer builds a wardrobe from rather than wears once. That rewards breadth, size availability and restock discipline over novelty. Pleated Story is already built for it: separates, XS–3XL, and roughly fifteen new styles a month.

Public industry research + network category patterns

02

Who is Pleated Story compared against?

Brands at this basket win on pieces per order and how often she comes back, not on price. Your own data says the appetite is there: 130 orders above ₹5,000 and 29 above ₹10,000 last month — while 53% of orders sit in a single ₹2,000–₹3,000 band. Both facts are true at once, and that gap is the opportunity.

03

Which creatives convert in this category?

Movement converts: drape, twirl and on-body wear beat flat-lay stills in ethnic wear — and your numbers agree. Instagram converts at 44.0% against Facebook's 33.8%, with better login and better payment completion at every step. What conversion should you expect?

05

Which offers preserve margin?

Threshold ladders and set-building beat blanket percentage-off, every time — and you are unusually disciplined already: not one variant in the catalogue renders a markdown, and the standing offer is a single flat 5% on first order. The margin-safe move is not a bigger discount. It is a shipping threshold above your ₹3,953 basket and a set price on the second piece.
