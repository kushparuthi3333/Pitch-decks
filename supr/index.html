Finding 1 · the payment page

More than a third of the shoppers who reach your payment page leave without choosing how to pay.

In the week to 12 October 1,949 shoppers reached the payment page and 1,222 picked a method; the other 727 left. Every other step in that funnel is healthy — 86% get from checkout to address, 99% from address to payment, and 91% of those who pick a method go on to order. So this is not a trust problem or a payments problem — it is a choosing problem. The levers are the order the options appear in, a reason to finish for someone about to leave — GoKwik's exit offer is switched off today — and the one-click coupon that removes code-hunting at the payment step, also off. Each tested on half your traffic.

Finding 2 · the one-tub basket

Finding 3 · the customers you already have

25,739 customers, 25,504 reachable on WhatsApp — and no welcome message, no win-back, not one saved audience.

If one message a month brings back one in two hundred of the 7,153 — our assumption — that is about 36 orders at your ₹3,398 basket, roughly ₹1.22L. We bank half, ₹61,000, because only customers who have agreed to hear from you can be messaged. This is the most conservative number in the deck.

Finding 4 · the standing discount

₹20.50L a month of discount, on 92.3% of orders — and GoKwik's discount report shows none of it.

In the thirty days to 12 October your orders carried ₹20.50L of discount, averaging ₹440 an order — up 59% on the thirty days before, against order growth of 48%. At nine orders in ten, that is not a promotion; it is your price. Meanwhile GoKwik's discount report reads zero, because the discount is applied before checkout ever sees it.

The consequence is not that it is too deep — we cannot say that, and we won't. It is that it cannot currently be varied, targeted or tested. You have an A/B test already built in your account, “5 vs 4 Prepaid Discount”, that would answer whether a point of it is doing any work — it has been switched off since April and has never run on a single cart. The first job is to move the discount somewhere it can be measured. Where it currently lives is the first question we need answered.
