Three of your four value packs cost more than buying the same bottles separately.

Checked against your own live prices on 22 September. And the ladder behind them runs the same way. Your A2 ghee 500ml is ₹1,798 a litre. Finding 2 · messaging

48% of the messages you send returned ₹12,645 between them.

WhatsApp is this business — 175,271 sends, 1,046 orders and ₹19.8L attributed in thirty days. But the twelve WhatsApp campaigns split cleanly in two. One of them, 9,505 messages, produced no orders at all. Add the 50,886-message email campaign that earned ₹3,609 and 79,714 of your 165,766 monthly sends returned ₹12,645.

The reason we are confident this is fixable: those five delivered fine — 70.9% to 87.5% — and all 113 of your live templates are approved, none rejected, none paused, unsubscribes at 0.06%. So the messages arrived and were read. A click rate of precisely zero on five templates, while seven others on the same list in the same month run 7.9–13.0%, is a broken link or missing tracking in those templates — not an audience problem. Underneath it sits the second half: you have exactly one marketing automation, live since 16 September, and it earns ₹30.23 per message against broadcast's ₹16.90. There is no abandoned cart, no welcome, no win-back and no replenishment reminder on a product people finish.

₹2.5L / month

The band, honestly: at a quarter of your own working templates' ₹22.96 per message, those 28,828 messages are worth ₹1.65L; at a third, ₹2.18L.
