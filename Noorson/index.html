Finding 1 · the big one

193 of August's 613 orders are confirmed not to have reached the customer — and 284 more have no recorded outcome at all.

Of August's 613 orders, 133 are confirmed delivered, 80 came back undelivered and 113 were cancelled; for the other 284 no outcome reached GoKwik. GoKwik flags one courier's tracking credential as expired — the most likely reason the share of orders with a known result fell from about half in August to under a third in September. Cash on delivery has been 84–90% of orders almost every week since July — and today no GoKwik rule acts on it: the two part-payment rules in your account have been paused since they were set up as tests in May 2025, cash on delivery is shown to nine in ten shoppers at the payment page on any basket up to ₹4,999, and in your GoKwik WhatsApp the confirmation for cash-on-delivery orders is switched off.

What we can already see is where the risk sits. GoKwik's risk score marked 139 August orders as high risk — 136 of them cash on delivery — and of the 84 with an outcome, 19 were delivered. Among tracked orders, the riskiest score bands came back several times as often as the safest; and orders with an address under 20 characters came back far more often than those with a full one. That is exactly the pattern a part-payment rule and an order-confirmation message are built for.
